Hello there

Hi, I'm Hannah, a Computer Science student who builds apps for real people

I'm in my third year of a BSc in Computer Science at the University of Kabianga in Kenya. I love building things that make daily life easier, especially for the people around me.

I came up with and led the build of UoK Xchange, a barter and marketplace platform that lets students across three campuses swap and sell items safely, with verified student emails and live chat. I also built SwiftBank, an Android banking app with money transfers, QR payments, bill payments and M-Pesa top-ups.

I'm still growing as a developer and I enjoy that. Every project teaches me something new, and I'm looking for opportunities such as internships, attachments and freelance work where I can keep building useful software.
